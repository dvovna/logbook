Your approvers see time only once you submit it.

## Submit your time

- **A week.** On **🕒 My time**, click **📤 Submit week (N)**. It sends the drafts and rejected entries of the week you are looking at. The button shows only when the week has some.
- **All of it.** On **Home**, click **📤 Submit for Approval (N)** under **Time Tracking**. It sends all your drafts and rejected entries.
- **One entry.** Open its menu on **My time** and choose **Submit**.
- **With the form.** Tick **Submit the week's time for approval** in **Fill in the week**.

You see a notice such as "✅ 3 time entries (6h) sent for approval."

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 📝 Draft | Logged, not submitted. Only you see it. |
| 📤 Submitted | Waiting for an approver. You can't change it. |
| ✅ Approved | Accepted. Locked. |
| ❌ Rejected | Sent back with a reason. You can fix it and submit again. |

## Fix a rejected entry

When an approver rejects an entry, you get a direct message: "❌ … rejected your time entry", with the project, date and hours, and where to fix it.

1. Open **🕒 My time** and go to the week of the entry. The entry shows **Reason:** with the approver's note.
2. Choose **Edit** in its menu, or change the cell in **Fill in the week**.
3. Submit it again with **Submit** or **📤 Submit week**.

Changing a rejected entry makes it a draft again.

## Older open entries

If drafts or rejected entries are in earlier weeks than the one shown, a line under the table says "📝 N open entries in earlier weeks" with a **Go to week of** … button that goes to the oldest such week.

## The Monday reminder

On Monday morning (UTC), Logbook sends a direct message to anyone with drafts or rejected entries from before that week. It counts the entries and hours, says how many were rejected and which weeks they are in, and points you to **Home → ✏️ Edit Entries** and **📤 Submit for Approval**.

## What approvers see

Approvers of your team, which are the managers who list it, get one direct message when you submit: who submitted, how many entries and hours, and on which projects. Their **✅ Approvals** screen lists the entries with **Approve** and **Reject** buttons, and **Approve all**. When your team has no manager to approve, HR sees them, or the admins when nobody has the HR role. HR and admins can decide anyone's entries from **All pending entries**. Nobody approves their own time.
