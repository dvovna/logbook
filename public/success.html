Logbook is installed

You are its admin. Setup happens in Slack: Logbook has sent you a message with the steps, and its Home tab shows a checklist until they are done.

What to do next

  1. Add a vacation policy. Days a year and public holidays. Without one, time off is tracked with no limits.
  2. Set the company rules. Who approves after the team lead, and who hears of decisions.
  3. Create teams and put people in them. One at a time, or all at once from a file.
  4. Bring in leave balances. Optional: what everyone has left, and the leave already agreed.
