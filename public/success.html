Logbook is installed

You are its admin. Setup happens in Slack: Logbook has sent you a message, and its Home tab shows a checklist of the steps until they are done.

What to do next

  1. Create a vacation policy. Days a year and public holidays. The first one applies to everyone.
  2. Set the company rules. Whether anyone approves after the team's approvers, and who hears of decisions.
  3. Create teams and put people in them. One at a time, or all at once from a file.
  4. Tell your team. Logbook gives you a ready message to post in a channel.
  5. Bring in leave balances. Optional: what everyone has left, and the leave already agreed.
